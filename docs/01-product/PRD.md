# YourTurn MVP product requirements

**Status:** Phase 1 documentation draft for YT-008; not implemented, approved contracts, payment authorization or release approval. Human authorization and coordinated ownership are recorded in `tasks/BOARD.md`, “Human Phase 1 authorization and coordinated ownership.” Author: Product Manager, phase1-product. UX hypotheses are in `USER_STORIES.md` (YT-012).

## Problem, audience and intended outcome

**Hypothesis:** existing friend circles may find it easier to arrange voluntary time together when organizing opportunities rotate and optional planning help reduces effort. This is not evidence of friendship improvement, fair burden or effective drift prediction. The MVP audience is fictional circles of 3–6 existing friends in an isolated synthetic demo; no stranger matching, real-person research or live service enrollment is authorized.

The intended outcome is understandable, voluntary planning outside the app, not engagement, contribution conversion or retention. A scripted edge change demonstrates software behavior only. Source boundaries: `docs/00-context/PRODUCT.md` §Requirement status; `CONSTRAINTS.md` §Non-negotiable safety limits; `docs/04-behavioral/PRINCIPLES.md` and `ETHICS_CHECKLIST.md` E-01–E-08. Behavioral evidence and claim permissions remain in `RESEARCH_NOTES.md`; this PRD adds no empirical claims.

## Role journeys

- **Invited member:** inspect synthetic/demo boundary and proposed contribution/consequences → accept membership or decline → independently choose tracking (off by default) → agree or decline the current amount → view circle readiness without pressure on dissenters. No charge or tracking follows invitation acceptance.
- **Current/upcoming host:** see their turn and conceptual budget → request three plans or use a fixed-list non-AI path → review/reject/defer → explicitly approve one exact version. Host-only guidance is positive and synthetic; becoming host creates neither consent nor access to private reasons. Approval does not claim a payment or reservation.
- **Non-host member:** see permitted shared plan information → optionally RSVP/skip without penalty → change tracking, pause or leave privately. No access to host-only pair guidance; no leaderboard or missed-attendance shame.
- **Paused or exiting member:** open discoverable private control → inspect approved consequences (or explicit policy-pending demo notice) → confirm/cancel. Participation access and provider collection states stay distinct; show pending/failure honestly, never imply an unverified provider cancellation.
- **Demo operator/reviewer:** select/reset synthetic fixtures → inspect a complete stable-membership rotation, consent withdrawal and failure cases → demonstrate explicitly scripted graph change. No real identities, credentials, personal histories or invented payment evidence enter fixtures.

## Proposed demo conventions, not policy decisions

These proposals make mock scenarios reproducible; Architect, Designer and Behavioral must reconcile them in YT-014 before implementation. They do not authorize money flow or resolve Q-001/Q-002/Q-006.

- **D-P01 — Amount validation:** propose positive whole cents through 1500, default 500, with agreement bound to amount and membership version. Minimum permitted contribution and any zero-cost mode are not specified by the brief; owner decision needed. A changed proposal invalidates prior agreement to that proposal, not historical payment evidence.
- **D-P02 — Rotation/exceptions:** propose stable accepted-member order with immutable member-ID tie-break and explicit fixture month advancement (no wall-clock automatic demo collection). Stable members host once per cycle. On pause/leave/join, preserve historical turns, invalidate stale approvals and suspend affected advancement until an approved reassignment rule exists. Do not trap a member in a circle to preserve rotation. Below three members, stop new funded planning; exit still works. Calendar/timezone, resume order, host decline and membership-change rules are unresolved.
- **D-P03 — Unavailable/non-AI:** propose one bounded regeneration retry after invalid output, then explicit unavailable. Allow browsing approved fixed demo venues or deferring without AI; the same budget/host/payment gates apply to any manually selected plan. No automatic selection, fake fourth plan or fabricated venue slot.
- **D-P04 — Pause/exit fixtures:** may illustrate a local participation-state change labelled “demo only; payment effects not executed.” No synthetic forfeiture, refund, subscription cancellation or successful pause-of-collection is evidence of provider behavior. Actual collection handling and peer-visible status require Q-006 resolution.

## Non-goals and ordered cuts

No real money, cash to hosts, production deployment, real-person graph/inference, contact import, stranger matching, validated friendship diagnosis, numeric real-person scores, public comparisons, hardship-reason collection, mandatory attendance, guilt notifications or autonomous spending. No external research recruitment/contact. No actual venue reservation integration, live venue search, automatic attendance confirmation or claim that capture means a booked event. No new subscriptions/payment implementation during this documentation phase.

Keep in LATER, in order: live venue search; turn-order optimization; Payouts refunds; memory recap. Refund/dispute rights still require review even though a Payouts feature is out of scope. Never silently cut the required genuine sandbox demonstration, synthetic drift illustration or behavioral safeguards. Missing credentials mean BLOCKED; an actual failed venue Order may trigger an ADR 0003 proposal, never an approved simulation fallback by default (Q-004).
