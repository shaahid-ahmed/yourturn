# Trust and safety

**Task:** YT-026, abuse and coercion assessment · **Role:** Trust & Safety (Vid, taken over from Alan on November 12, 2026) · **Status:** Phase 1 assessment for review. **Not an approved policy, a moderation system, or a launch sign-off.**

**Scope.** Unspoken is used by **existing friend groups of 3–8**, and the risks are inside those groups. People can pressure one another about money, plans and who is in or out. This document lists those scenarios against the product **as built on `main`**, and names a control, a gap, an owner and a test for each. Controls were checked in code; where a control is only a design intent, the document says so.

**Inputs:** the [UX spec](../02-design/source/Unspoken%20Experience%20and%20Pages%20Spec.pdf), [FLOWS.md](../02-design/FLOWS.md), [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md) (including the UN-009 boundary), [THREAT_MODEL.md](THREAT_MODEL.md), [ETHICS_CHECKLIST.md](../04-behavioral/ETHICS_CHECKLIST.md), ADR 0007, ADR 0008, and the Q-011 decisions on the board.

**Product facts this rests on.** One member (the volunteer payer) pays the venue directly; the app only tracks what the others owe them (ADR 0008). Votes and ready-check answers are visible **with names** (spec, Q-013). Private limits and stretch asks are visible only to the member and their planner. There is no pot, no score, no ranking and no friendship inference.

Severity: **High** means it can cost a member money, or expose them, without their consent. **Medium** means social harm that the product makes easier. **Low** means social harm the product barely touches.

## Retaliatory ranking

There is nothing to rank. No score, streak, reliability rating, "most generous" or leaderboard exists, and the P23 rating goes only to the member's own planner (spec; built). **Requirement:** keep it that way. Any feature that compares members is a scope change that needs Behavioral sign-off (ETHICS_CHECKLIST).

## Moderation boundaries

- **The demo has no moderation, no reports and no admin.** Every person in the demo is synthetic. This is acceptable only because no real users exist.
- **Before real users**, the minimum is: delete your own message; block a person (they stop seeing you in invites); and report a message. A report would send only the message, the pod id and the time to a human reviewer, never the chat history, limits or balances. This is a **proposal for LATER**, not demo scope (RULES 4).
- **No automated content scanning** of pod or private chats. It would contradict the privacy model, and nothing in the spec asks for it.
- **Escalation:** in the demo, issues go to the board. A real launch needs a named human contact and response times, which is a human decision.

## Summary

- **High, open:** TS-01 (the payer can raise the total without limit or consent), TS-12 (no frequency limit on stretch asks), and TS-16 (the `STRETCH_ASKS` state is visible to the pod).
- **Medium:** mostly mitigated by built controls; the open items are decisions (Q-013, Q-014, PV-03) and copy.
- **Not in demo scope:** reporting, blocking and message deletion. They are listed for LATER and required before any real user.
