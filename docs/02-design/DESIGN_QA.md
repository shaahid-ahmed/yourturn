# Unspoken design QA

**Task:** US-205 · **Owner:** Product Designer (Vid) · **Date:** November 12, 2026 · **Build:** `apps/web` from PR #53 (US-101–US-105), plus the US-204 accessibility fixes (PR #64).

## What "pixel comparison" means here

The source, [Unspoken Experience and Pages Spec](source/Unspoken%20Experience%20and%20Pages%20Spec.pdf), is a **text specification with one flow diagram**. It has no screen mockups, so there are no source pixels to diff against. Instead, each built screen was rendered at 390×844 (and 320px for reflow) and compared against three things:

1. **What the spec puts on that page.** Its per-page bullets on pages 5–13, and its rules on pages 1–3 and 13–15.
2. **The screen and state specification** in [FLOWS.md](FLOWS.md).
3. **The visual system** in [DESIGN_SYSTEM.md](DESIGN_SYSTEM.md) and `tokens.css`: colour, type, touch, the private surface, the honesty labels, and PayPal blue as line art only.

Every deviation is either **fixed** in this task or **recorded** below with an owner. Screenshots can be regenerated with `tests/a11y/app-audit.mjs`. `tests/web-design-qa.test.mjs` guards the fixes in CI.
